Common questions

Where are my wallpapers stored?

In Wallnetic's library on your Mac. Imported videos are copied into it, and your original files are never changed. Removing a wallpaper in Wallnetic deletes only its copy.

Can Wallnetic play video on the lock screen?

No. macOS does not let apps play video on the lock screen. Wallnetic can set a still frame of your wallpaper as the system wallpaper instead, which macOS shows on the lock screen and in Mission Control.

Why did my wallpaper pause?

Wallnetic pauses when you pause it and while the Mac is locked, a screen saver runs or the display sleeps. Depending on your settings, it also pauses on battery power, in Low Power Mode or for a fullscreen app. It resumes when the reason ends.
