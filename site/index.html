Your originals are never changed. Trim a loop or make an optimized copy without touching the file.

Every display, its own wallpaper

Give each display a different video, or the same one on all. Plan the day on a schedule timeline and give playlist items their own timing.

Calm when it should be

Wallnetic pauses while your Mac is locked or asleep and, by default, behind fullscreen apps. Pause After stops motion after a time you choose; Calm Mode holds it with one click. Playback profiles set the frame rate.

No account, no ads, no tracking: the App Store lists Wallnetic as Data Not Collected. The whole app is on GitHub.

Common questions.

Does Wallnetic work on Intel Macs?

Yes. The same app runs natively on Intel and Apple silicon Macs with macOS 13 Ventura or later, from the Mac App Store or as a direct download from GitHub.

Is it really free?

Yes. Wallnetic is free on the Mac App Store, with no account, ads or subscription, and its source code is open on GitHub.

Where do the wallpapers come from?

From you. Import videos you own, browse wallpaper sites in Discover, or download Wallnetic's free packs. Each website has its own license terms for the videos it offers.

Can the wallpaper move on the lock screen?

No. macOS doesn't let apps play video on the lock screen. Wallnetic can set a still frame of your wallpaper as the system wallpaper, which macOS shows on the lock screen and in Mission Control.

Will a moving wallpaper get in my way?

It stays below your icons and windows and pauses while the Mac is locked, asleep or showing a screen saver. You can also have it pause on battery, behind fullscreen apps, after a set time, or with Calm Mode.

Does Wallnetic collect my data?

No. Your library and settings stay on your Mac. The privacy policy describes the few optional features that use the network.

Is Wallnetic available in my language?

The app is in English. Its App Store page is also available in French, German and Simplified Chinese.
