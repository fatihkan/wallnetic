Status: release candidate; not yet uploaded or submitted for review.

- Marketing version: **1.4.2**
- Candidate build: **11** (confirm against App Store Connect before uploading)
- Bundle identifiers: `com.wallnetic.app`, `com.wallnetic.app.WallneticWidget`
- Deployment target: macOS 13.0; archive both arm64 and x86_64.
- Keep App Sandbox and Hardened Runtime enabled in the distribution archive.
- Supply the signing team through Xcode or command-line settings; do not commit
  a development team or credentials into the project.

## What's New — English

This update improves wallpaper playback and the everyday library experience.

- More reliable wallpaper loading, looping and switching, with improved video proportions.
- Window controls and first-launch setup improvements for macOS Tahoe.
- Fixes for gallery previews, library imports, search and Settings.
- Better handling of pause, wake and interrupted video loads.
- Stability improvements when opening links and importing downloaded wallpapers.
