## What's New — English

This update gives you more control over wallpaper motion and new ways to shape
your wallpapers.

- Trim a video to loop just the part you like, and add an optional clock and date to your wallpaper, shown in English with your region's date and time formats.
- More control over motion: stop it after a time you choose with Pause After, hold it with Calm Mode, pick a playback profile, or have it pause when your Mac gets hot. The menu bar shows each display's playback status and why it is paused.
- Wallpapers now play at up to 30 frames per second by default; choose Quality in Settings > Playback for the smoothest motion.
- Wallnetic no longer switches wallpapers automatically for each Space; your earlier Space choices are kept in Settings > Spaces, where you can apply them yourself.
- Plan your day on a schedule timeline and give each playlist wallpaper its own duration. Your time-of-day settings carry over.
- Try an included sample from the welcome tour, manage Library space in the new Storage settings, make optimized copies of videos, and download optional wallpaper packs in Discover.
- Fixes: HDR videos no longer look washed out with the Metal renderer, widgets follow your 12- or 24-hour setting, and per-display wallpapers come back after a restart.

## Review notes

This update adds playback controls (Pause After, Calm Mode, playback profiles
and optional thermal-aware playback), loop ranges (Trim), an optional desktop
clock that is off by default (Settings > Effects > Desktop Clock), daily
schedules, a Storage page, optimized copies, a sample video in the welcome tour
and optional wallpaper packs. The macOS 13 minimum, bundle identifiers,
entitlements and permission prompts are unchanged. The privacy manifest now
also declares System Boot Time (35F9.1, to measure elapsed time for frame pacing
and timers) and Disk Space (E174.1, to check free space before writing an
optimized copy).

Every network request is started by the user. Wallpaper packs are fetched from
the public GitHub repository only after Refresh Catalog or Download Pack in
Discover > Browse Packs; the welcome tour's sample is bundled and works offline.

Automatic per-Space wallpaper switching was removed because individual Spaces
cannot be identified reliably. Earlier Space choices remain as saved choices
that the user applies in Settings > Spaces.

To try the main additions: choose **Try a sample** on the first page of the
welcome tour (Settings > General > Show welcome tour and sample reopens it);
Calm Mode is in the menu bar and on Command–Shift–M while Wallnetic is active;
Trim… is in a Library item's context menu.

## Upgrade notes

These follow from the code and belong in the upgrade check of the collective
session; none has been observed on a device yet.

- Playback profiles default to Balanced, so the Metal renderer shows at most 30
  frames per second until Quality is chosen. The CHANGELOG says so.
- Per-display choices made in 1.4.2 were keyed by IDs that change on every
  launch, so they are not carried over: an upgraded Mac starts in Same on all
  displays.
- Time-of-day slots become schedule ranges; saved Space choices show Needs
  reassignment and are never applied automatically.
- A manual Pause now lasts until Play; with the Metal renderer a wallpaper
  chosen while paused appears when Play is pressed.

## GitHub distribution

The `v1.5.0` release is to package the same app source and **1.5.0 (12)**
version as the App Store release into separate Apple Silicon and Intel DMGs,
with `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Direct-download DMGs use ad-hoc signatures, are not
notarized and do not run sandboxed. A draft is verified before publication.
