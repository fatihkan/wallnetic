# Wallnetic 1.6.0 — release plan

Status: **draft, not released.** Everything below describes `dev`. No version
number has been set in the project, nothing is tagged and nothing has been
submitted.

- Marketing version: **1.6.0** (proposed)
- Build: **13** (proposed; the last App Store Connect build is 1.5.0 / 12)
- Bundle identifiers: `com.wallnetic.app`, `com.wallnetic.app.WallneticWidget`
- Deployment target: macOS 13.0; archive both arm64 and x86_64.
- Keep App Sandbox and Hardened Runtime enabled in the distribution archive.
- Supply the signing team through Xcode or command-line settings; do not commit
  a development team or credentials into the project.

The full list of changes is in the [CHANGELOG](../../CHANGELOG.md) under
Unreleased.

## What's New — English

A draft. Remove the languages line, or shorten its list, to match the
languages that ship.

A new look, and more ways to decide when your wallpaper moves.

- A fresh interface: Home now starts with what each display is showing, the main views are tabs in the toolbar, and on macOS 26 the app uses Liquid Glass.
- Preview a wallpaper before you apply it, with its trim, scaling, effects and clock.
- Fit with Blurred Backdrop shows the whole video on any display, with no cropping and no black bars.
- Playback Rules pause your wallpaper while chosen apps are in front or running, when your Mac is idle, on low battery, or on the displays you are not using.
- The Dynamic Island now flows out of the notch on MacBooks and sits in the menu bar on other displays.
- Wallnetic now speaks German, French, Japanese and Simplified Chinese.
- Fixes: Settings opens in a single window, and source descriptions in Discover are no longer cut off.

## Review notes

A draft for App Review.

This update refreshes the interface (toolbar tabs, a Home that shows the
current desktop, Liquid Glass on macOS 26) and adds a preview sheet, a second
scaling mode, optional playback rules, a redesigned Dynamic Island that is off
by default (Settings > General > Dynamic Island), and translations of the
interface.

- The macOS 13 minimum, bundle identifiers and entitlements are unchanged.
- **No new permission prompt.** The playback rules read the frontmost and
  running apps, the time since the last input event, the power source, the
  pointer's position and window bounds. None of these asks the user for
  access.
- To confirm before submission: that the privacy manifest needs no new
  required-reason entry for the idle rule and the power source.

## Upgrade notes

These follow from the code. None has been observed on an upgraded Mac yet.

- Every new feature is off or unchanged by default: scaling stays Fill, the
  five playback rules are off, the Dynamic Island keeps its setting.
- A Mac whose first language is German, French, Japanese or Simplified
  Chinese starts in that language after the update. The schedule's range
  names (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night) are the user's own text and stay
  as they are.
- The Dynamic Island no longer has rename, favorite, shuffle and previous.

- **Unchanged unless decided otherwise:** names, subtitles, keywords,

## GitHub distribution

As for 1.5.0: the `v1.6.0` release packages the same source and version into
separate Apple Silicon and Intel DMGs with `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Direct-download
DMGs use ad-hoc signatures, are not notarized and do not run sandboxed.
